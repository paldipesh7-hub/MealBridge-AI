# MealBridge AI

**AI-Powered Food Rescue & Intelligent Redistribution Platform**

Connecting surplus food with people who need it.

## Overview

MealBridge AI is an India-first platform designed to connect food donors
with verified NGOs and recipient organizations.

The platform aims to simplify surplus food donation, intelligent recipient
matching, pickup coordination, monetary contributions and impact tracking.

## Core Modules

- MealBridge Rescue: Food donation and redistribution.
- MealBridge Give: Monetary donations and fundraising campaigns.
- MealBridge Intelligence: AI-assisted matching and analytics.
